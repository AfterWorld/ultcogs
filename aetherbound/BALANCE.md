# Phase 1 balance baseline

6,750 seeded solo simulations; fixed monster level, equally leveled player, no potions. Each player allocates one point/level to their class stat and one to vitality. The scripted strategy interrupts charged attacks and uses its damage skill when available. Starter gear is level 1 in every slot; common/rare gear matches the player level.

These checks establish bounds, not final balance. They do not simulate human mistakes, party combat, or the full acquisition economy. Live Discord playtesting is still required.
